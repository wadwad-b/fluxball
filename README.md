# Fluxball

[![Godot](https://img.shields.io/badge/Godot-478CBF?style=for-the-badge&logo=godotengine&logoColor=white)](https://godotengine.org/)

Fluxball is a fast-paced 2D two player platformer/physics soccer-style game. Take control of your player, run across the field, and make your best attempt to score in your opponent's goal. Don't be afraid to use your ability to phase through the ball and gain a speed boost.

Play here: https://wadwad-b.itch.io/fluxball

## Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Jump | W | Up Arrow |
| Move left | A | Left Arrow |
| Move right | D | Right Arrow |
| Activate ability | S | Down Arrow |
